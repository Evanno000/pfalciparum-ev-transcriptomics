# Duplicate Transcript Length Analysis

## Objective
To investigate whether genes with identical expression profiles
across the 140 samples also have identical annotated transcript lengths.

## Method
The transcript lengths of all genes in the 13 previously identified
duplicate-expression groups were compared using PlasmoDB annotations.

## Results
- Total duplicate-expression groups: 13
- Total genes investigated: 30
- Groups with identical transcript lengths: 13
- Groups with different transcript lengths: 0

All genes within each duplicate-expression group had identical
annotated transcript lengths.

## Interpretation
The identical transcript lengths provide an additional observation
supporting the similarity among genes within these groups.

However, identical transcript lengths do not establish identical
nucleotide sequences or explain the identical expression profiles.

Further investigation of transcript sequences and the original
quantification methodology is required.

## Conclusion
All 13 duplicate-expression groups showed identical annotated
transcript lengths within their respective groups.
